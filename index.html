Fix it

Sound or picture glitched after switching?

Re-locks onto the input you last picked. Fixes most blank screens and audio drop-outs without going home.

TV feeling slow?

Clears the Roku's memory cache. The screen freezes for about 15 seconds, then the TV restarts on its own. Safe to do once a week.

Cut the bloat

Roku locks its own software, so these have to be switched on the TV with the regular remote. Each one takes under a minute. Your ticks are saved on this phone.

TV address

If the remote stops reaching the TV, check the address in Settings → Network → About on the TV. To stop it changing, reserve it in your router's settings.
